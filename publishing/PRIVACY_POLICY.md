# fullpage privacy policy draft

Proposed disclosure for version 1.4.0. Owner must approve and supply publisher identity, contact information and an effective date before publication.

fullpage processes screenshots locally in Firefox. It does not operate a screenshot server and does not send captures, page text, browsing history or settings to an extension-operated service. No analytics, advertising integration, account or tracking identifier is included.

When you initiate capture, fullpage temporarily accesses the selected page and permitted embedded frames to measure and scroll content, adjust layout and assemble screenshot pixels. Optional website access may be requested to capture cross-origin frame contents. Captured pages may make their own ordinary network or lazy-loading requests while being viewed or scrolled; those websites have their own policies.

Your format, naming, timing, sound and popup preferences are stored in Firefox's local extension storage. Temporary progress is held in session storage. Optional origin permissions and download records are managed by Firefox. The extension keeps no capture gallery or history database. Output files remain in the destination you select or Firefox's configured Downloads folder until you delete them.

You can change preferences in Settings and revoke optional site access in Firefox's add-on permissions. Uninstalling normally clears the extension's stored preferences, subject to developer/browser retention settings. Uninstalling does not delete downloaded captures. Do not share a capture containing personal information unless you intend to disclose it.

For questions, use the publisher support contact shown on the final Firefox Add-ons listing. This draft must be updated before any future feature introduces uploads, cloud storage, analytics or other data processing.
