# Store listing draft

Status: new handoff draft for owner review; not submitted or approved.

Name: fullpage

Summary: Capture scrolling pages locally as PNG, JPEG or a single long-page PDF.

Description:

Click the fullpage camera icon to capture a scrolling webpage and save it on your device. Choose lossless PNG, JPEG or a single-page image PDF. Follow progress in a compact popup and stop capture when needed.

fullpage handles ordinary vertical and horizontal page overflow, eligible vertically scrolling panels, and accessible embedded frames. Sticky and fixed headers are adjusted to reduce repeated headers in the result. Cross-origin frames may ask for access to their website before full capture.

Choose filename components, scroll timing, image waits, save-dialog behavior, popup auto-close and shutter sound in Settings. No account, analytics, capture service or screenshot uploads are used. Settings stay local.

Requires Firefox desktop 140 or newer. Protected pages, virtualized content, unusual dynamic layouts and independently scrolling horizontal panels can be unsupported. Nested/custom scrollbar suppression is not universal. PDFs are one raster image on one long page, not searchable text.

Suggested category: Photos, Music & Videos or an appropriate screenshot/productivity category offered by the current portal. Owner must select the final category, support contact/URL, author identity and license. No support address or license is invented here. Obtain current UI screenshots from Firefox; historical test-output images are not a substitute for current store UI screenshots.
